<h2 class="cv-section-title" id="open-science"><span>Open Science</span></h2>

My [GitHub profile](https://github.com/Jeff97) provides open research resources:

* **Paper-associated repositories:** ABAQUS input files, Fortran UMAT subroutines and practical run instructions for reproducing representative numerical results.
* **[FEM coding tutorials](https://github.com/Jeff97/FEM-Codes):** Stepwise development of finite-element implementations through worked Fortran examples, from basis functions and numerical integration to 2D/3D linear and nonlinear formulations.

Together, these resources support verification, reuse and accessible FEM skills development within the computational-mechanics community.
