I am currently an **Assistant Researcher (Postdoc)** at the School of Civil Engineering and Transportation, **South China University of Technology (SCUT)**, working with **Prof. Xiaohu Yao**.

I received my Ph.D. in Mechanics from SCUT in 2023 and was a visiting Ph.D. student at Swansea University (2021.11-2022.11), co-supervised by **Prof. Mokarram Hossain**.

My research interests focus on **Soft Matter Mechanics**, **Plate and Shell Theory**, **Growth-Induced Deformation and Shape Control**, **Multi-field Coupled Mechanics**, **Mixed Finite Element Methods**, and **ABAQUS Subroutine Development**.

## Research Highlights

### 1. Analytical Modeling of Growth-Induced Deformations in Plates and Shells
We developed theoretical frameworks for growth-induced deformations in hyperelastic plates and shells. Starting from 3D governing equations and using asymptotic/perturbation methods, we derived analytical results for bending, buckling, and post-buckling behaviors, providing insights into instability mechanisms and pattern formation.

### 2. Stress-Free Growth-Based Shape Programming of Hyperelastic Structures
![Shape Control](/images/ShapeControl.jpg)
We proposed a stress-free growth approach for precise shape programming. By deriving analytical relationships between growth tensors and target geometries, we enabled conformal mappings and general shape transformations of thin hyperelastic shells, supporting complex surface generation.

### 3. Numerical Simulation of Multi-field Coupled Growth Deformation
![Volvox inversion](/images/Volvox.png)
We developed finite element frameworks for coupled growth behaviors in soft materials, including electro-mechanical and magneto-mechanical settings. By establishing coupled 3D governing systems and implementing mixed formulations in ABAQUS, we simulated surface pattern modulation and biological morphogenesis (e.g., Volvox inversion), demonstrating potential for adaptive soft structures.
